almustafawelfaretrust

UK charity, founded 1983 · Brentford, London

Al Mustafa Welfare Trust is a UK charity founded in 1983, working to lift people out of poverty through Sadaqah Jariyah and Zakat funded projects. We build water wells and hand pumps, sponsor orphans and Hafiz students, support education and healthcare, restore sight through cataract surgery, and deliver emergency relief where it is needed most. From our base in Brentford, London, we work with communities across Pakistan, Bangladesh, Africa and beyond. Give your Zakat and Sadaqah today and help change a life.
